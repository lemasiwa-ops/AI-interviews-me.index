I am an Optometrist, Academic and Leader with fifteen years of clinical experience and a decade in academia.

My work brings together clinical eye care, research, education, advocacy and organisational leadership. A solid foundation in science and a broad range of leadership roles have allowed me to actively increase access to health care services and tertiary education and contribute to transforming organisations.

I am keen on opportunities that allow me a wider reach and deeper impact.

Increasing access to eye care is a key part of my life purpose. My clinical work includes comprehensive eye care for the general public, including geriatric and paediatric patients.

  • Fifteen years of clinical experience across comprehensive eye care, with experience serving people from all walks of life.
  • Founding Director of Eye Do and The Village Eye Attic, where increasing access to eye care is a key part of my life purpose.
  • Clinical leadership encompassing team development, in-house training, business strategy and implementation for increased impact.

My academic work combines teaching, curriculum development, research collaboration, supervision and evidence synthesis.

  • Spearheaded the introduction of the first BSc Optometry curriculum in Zimbabwe, from advocacy and content development through accreditation and delivery of lectures.
  • Research Fellow at the University of Zimbabwe, with experience coordinating international research teams, ethical clearance, grant applications and research record keeping.
  • Experience delivering theoretical and clinical modules at tertiary level and serving as a supervisor and examiner of undergraduate and postgraduate students.
  • Advanced evidence synthesis to inform best practices is a strong quality I bring to academic and research work.

My leadership experience spans academia, professional advocacy, governance and organisational strategy.

  • Board-level experience in tertiary education, including service as Vice Chairperson of the Higher Education Examinations Council in Zimbabwe.
  • Advocacy focused on increasing awareness and access to optometry services and contributing to dialogue around the scope of optometry practice in Zimbabwe.
  • Experience in strategy development, corporate governance, regulatory compliance, policy implementation and organisational decision-making.
  • A professional speaker who regularly presents at conferences on topics linked to specialist interests in Optometry.

I am open to opportunities where my experience in clinical optometry, academia, research and leadership can contribute to meaningful work and wider impact.
